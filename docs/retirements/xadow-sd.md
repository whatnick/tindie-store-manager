# Xadow SD retirement notice

Effective **2026-11-08**, Xadow SD (`XADOW-SD`,
[Tindie product 7348](https://www.tindie.com/products/7348/)) is retired from
the whatnick product range. No further manufacturing or restocking is planned.

## Rationale and evidence

Xadow SD belongs to the legacy original-Xadow accessory ecosystem. Seeed
Studio's [Xadow product-lines discussion](https://forum.seeedstudio.com/t/xadow-product-lines/18930)
is classified under **End-Of-Life Products & Services / Wearables**.
In its reply dated 22 December 2015, Seeed stated that it was migrating the
expansion boards to an 11-pin bus, in response to a question about the original
12-pin product line.

This platform transition and its end-of-life classification justify retiring
our legacy accessory rather than committing inventory and development effort
to it. This is not a blanket claim about the availability of every Xadow v2
product.

Seeed's related [Xadow Storage product page](https://www.seeedstudio.com/Xadow-Storage-p-1263.html)
showed **Out of stock** when checked on 2026-11-08. Out-of-stock status alone
does not establish discontinuation; the lifecycle rationale above is based
on the original platform's history, not solely on availability.

## Existing users and new designs

Existing working modules need not be replaced. Historical product information
remains available. For new designs, choose a supported SD-card interface module
appropriate to the host, voltage levels and firmware. Do not assume connector
or electrical compatibility with another Xadow generation or a generic module.

The local catalogue is marked inactive/retired and the product is tracked in
Decommission on the lifecycle project. Updating and retiring the live Tindie
listing requires an authenticated seller session; the local retirement is not
proof that the marketplace listing has changed.
