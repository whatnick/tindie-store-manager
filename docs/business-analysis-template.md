# Hardware business analysis template

Use this template for every electronic or mechanical product version. Keep
measured facts separate from estimates and assumptions. A retirement decision
is a proposal until the owner explicitly approves it.

## Decision summary

- **Lifecycle stage:**
- **Proposed decision:** Invest / Sustain / Revise / Consolidate / Retirement candidate / Retired
- **Approval state:** Proposed / Approved
- **Evidence confidence:** High / Medium / Low
- **Next review date:**

## Product and evidence boundary

State the exact hardware version, included parts, sales channels and evidence
date. Link the design repository, product listing and manufacturing package.

## Demand evidence

Record lifetime units and orders, most recent sale, recent channel activity,
stock, enquiries, returns and known customer use. Do not include customer
names, addresses or email addresses.

## Cost and pricing

Show unit cost at the intended batch size, fees, freight, assembly/test labour,
rework allowance and contribution margin. Include quantity breaks when useful.

## Alternatives and portfolio role

Compare supported replacements, adjacent products and internal cannibalisation.
State whether alternatives are drop-in replacements.

## Market and competition

Separate sourced market facts from assumptions. Include realistic reachable
customers, competing products, differentiation and a testable price range.

## Recommendation and gates

List the evidence required before the next stage. For a retirement candidate,
include remaining stock, customer migration guidance, documentation retention,
listing changes and the explicit owner-approval gate.
